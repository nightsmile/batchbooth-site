Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026 · Applies to the BatchBooth app for iPhone

The short version

  • Your photos stay on your iPhone. Every step — cutout, background, resize, export — runs on the device. Nothing is uploaded, and we never see your pictures.
  • What we do collect: your purchase history (to know whether your subscription is active), anonymous usage statistics (which screens and features are used, which marketplaces you export to, an approximate country or city derived from your IP address) and crash and performance reports. All of it is tied to random app IDs, not to you.
  • No account, no email, no advertising ID, no tracking across apps, no ads, no selling data.

1. Who we are

BatchBooth is published by an independent developer (“the developer of BatchBooth”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains what the BatchBooth app for iPhone (“the app”) does with information about you. If anything here is unclear, email leojiahk@outlook.com.

2. Your photos and items

The app exists to process your item photos, and it does that entirely on your device:

  • Cutouts, background replacement, shadows, cropping, resizing and file export all run locally using Apple’s on-device frameworks.
  • Your photos, cutouts, item names, templates and export history are stored in the app’s own folder on your iPhone. They are never uploaded, never queued on a server and never accessible to us.
  • Deleting the app deletes that folder and everything in it.
  • If you allow it, finished exports are also saved to your Photos library in albums named after each item. Those albums belong to your Photos library and you can delete them at any time.

Photo library permission

The app asks for access to your photo library and uses the wording below in the system prompt. You can change the permission at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos. If you grant limited or no access, the app copies the photos you pick into its own folder so it still works, but it cannot save exports to your library.

  • Read and write: “BatchBooth opens the photos you pick so it can reload the originals of your items later, and saves finished listing photos into albums named after each item.”
  • Add only: “BatchBooth saves your finished listing photos into albums named after each item.”

3. What we collect

The app uses the network for two jobs only: to verify purchases and subscriptions, and to collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports. For the first it uses Apple’s StoreKit and RevenueCat, a subscription-management service operated by RevenueCat, Inc. (United States). For the second it uses Google Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring), operated by Google LLC (United States). None of these services ever receives your photos, cutouts, item names or file names. Through them we collect exactly the following:

Purchase data and the usage attribute are attached to a random, anonymous app user ID generated by RevenueCat on your device the first time the app runs; it is shown on the app’s Privacy screen (Settings → Privacy). Firebase data is attached to its own random app-instance ID. Neither ID is derived from your name, email, phone number or Apple ID, we never link them to you, and we never combine them with other data to identify you.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts or precise location, and we do not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, and the app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission because it has no reason to.
  • We do not show ads, we do not send usage data to advertising networks, and we do not sell, rent or share your data for advertising.
  • There is no sign-up, account or password in the app.

Statistics that stay on your phone

The app also keeps a few aggregate counters locally (for example, how many free photos you have used) to show you your remaining free allowance. Those counters stay on your device.

4. Third parties

  • Apple processes all payments through the App Store and provides StoreKit, which the app uses to confirm purchases. Apple’s handling of your purchase and account data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (United States) receives the purchase history and the single usage attribute described above, tied to the anonymous ID, and stores them on our behalf. RevenueCat acts as our service provider and is bound by RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.
  • Google LLC (United States) provides Firebase and receives the usage statistics, approximate location, crash, performance and diagnostic data described above. Google processes this data on our behalf as a data processor under the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms; see also Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google’s Privacy Policy. We have not enabled Google signals or any advertising link in Firebase.

Because RevenueCat and Google operate in the United States, this data may be processed outside the country you live in. Where EU, UK or similar data-protection law applies, such transfers rely on these providers’ standard contractual clauses and other safeguards.

We use no advertising networks, attribution or ad-measurement SDKs, or social-media plug-ins.

5. How long we keep data

  • On your device: photos, items, templates and local statistics stay until you delete them or delete the app.
  • With RevenueCat: the purchase history and the usage attribute are kept, under the anonymous ID, until you ask us to delete them (see below). Deleting the app from your phone does not automatically delete this record, because it is what lets you restore a purchase later.
  • With Google Firebase: usage statistics are kept for no longer than 14 months; crash reports are kept for 90 days. They are then deleted automatically.
  • With Apple: your App Store purchase and subscription records are kept by Apple under Apple’s own retention rules; we cannot delete those.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Brazil or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. We honour these requests for everyone, wherever you live.

To exercise any of them, email leojiahk@outlook.com and include the anonymous ID shown on the app’s Privacy screen (Settings → Privacy), so we can find your record — it is the only way we can link a request to the data, since we hold nothing else that identifies you. Firebase statistics cannot be linked to that ID; to delete them, we delete the app’s analytics data on request or it expires as described above. We respond within 30 days. Requests about Apple’s own purchase records should go to Apple.

7. Children

BatchBooth is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Your photos never leave your device, which removes most risk by design. The purchase, usage and diagnostic data that does leave is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to Apple, RevenueCat and Google, and is never combined with information that could identify you.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date at the top of this page. For material changes we will also say so in the app’s release notes on the App Store. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact
