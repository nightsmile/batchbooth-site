Support

BatchBooth is a listing-photo workbench for people who sell second-hand items on more than one marketplace. You import one set of photos per item, and the app exports a correctly sized set for each platform you pick — all processed on your iPhone.

Need help or found a bug? Email leojiahk@outlook.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version; the “Send Feedback” button in the app’s Settings adds them for you. We usually reply within two business days.

Getting started

Which marketplaces are supported?

BatchBooth ships with export presets for eBay, Poshmark, Depop, Mercari and Vinted, and you can create your own templates (aspect ratio, long edge, photo limit, PNG or JPEG, background colour, shadow) for anything else.

Marketplace names are used only to describe which preset matches which site. BatchBooth is an independent app and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any of these marketplaces. The app does not post listings for you — you upload the exported photos yourself.

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Cutouts, backgrounds, resizing and export all run on your device. Your photos are never uploaded, never queued on a server and never seen by us. The only time the app talks to the internet is to check whether your subscription is active. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Where do my exported photos go?

Exports are written to BatchBooth’s own folder on your iPhone. You can browse them in the Files app under On My iPhone → BatchBooth, share them from the export screen, or AirDrop them.

If you have allowed BatchBooth to add photos to your library, each export is also saved to a Photos album named after the item, so you can pick them straight from the marketplace app’s photo picker.

Why does the Vinted preset only crop and resize?

Vinted does not allow edited or retouched item photos. For that preset BatchBooth deliberately skips cutouts, background replacement and shadows for the whole item and only crops and scales each photo to the right size, so what you upload is still the original picture. Other presets apply the full treatment to the cover photo and keep the detail shots untouched except for sizing.

What does “cover” and “detail” mean?

The first photo of an item is the cover: it gets a cutout, a clean background and the platform’s crop. Every other photo is a detail shot and is only cropped and resized, keeping the original pixels so buyers see the real condition of the item. You can change which photo is the cover at any time.

Which devices and languages are supported?

iPhone running iOS 17.0 or later. The app can also run on iPad and Apple silicon Macs in iPhone compatibility mode, but it is designed and tested for iPhone.

Available in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Simplified Chinese and Japanese. The app follows your system language; you can also pick a language just for BatchBooth in Settings.

Free use, plans and billing

What can I do for free?

Everything, up to a free allowance that is counted per photo exported — by default 8 photos. Editing is always free; the allowance is only checked when you export.

  • Exporting the same photo to several marketplaces counts as one photo.
  • Re-exporting a photo you have already used does not count again.

How do I restore a purchase on a new phone?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases inside the app, or tap “Restore Purchases” at the bottom of the plans screen. Make sure the phone is signed in with the same Apple account you bought with.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside the app. On your iPhone open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → BatchBooth, or use the “Manage Subscription” shortcut in the app’s Settings. To avoid being charged for the next period, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period (or the free trial) ends. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple too. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with the Apple account you bought with, and choose the BatchBooth purchase. We cannot issue refunds ourselves.

Troubleshooting

The app says a size is “community-sourced”. What does that mean?

Some marketplaces do not publish official image sizes. Where a preset’s size comes from seller experience rather than the platform’s own documentation, the app labels it so you know. If a marketplace rejects an export, please tell us which platform and what it said — we update presets as platforms change.

Import or export fails, or the app can’t see my photos.

Check iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → BatchBooth. With “Limited” or “None” access the app copies the photos you pick into its own folder and still works, but it cannot save exports to your Photos library. Free up storage if you have less than a few hundred megabytes left, then try again. If the problem continues, email us with the steps you took.

How do I delete my data?

Your photos, items and exports live only on your phone: deleting the app removes them. For the anonymous purchase record kept for subscription verification, see “Your rights” in the Privacy Policy.
